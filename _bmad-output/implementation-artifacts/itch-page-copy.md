## The Debugger 0x03: Wild Shift

**Shift your weapon's bits mid-fight to squash monstrous bugs.**

[COVER GIF: to capture]
<!-- H-S79-1: a shift into a Dash Strike crit, <= 15 s, <= 8 MB. -->

A developer and their rubber duck fall into a fantasy RPG whose monsters are software bugs. Your weapon is a bit register: a ring of ability bits you rotate left (`<<`) or right (`>>`) in real time. The two leftmost bits are your two action buttons, and pressing them in order fires that window's combo. Three bosses, Infinite Loop, Null Pointer and Segfault, each fight like the bug they are named after. Every defeated bug drops a new bit into the register and changes which combos exist. 1-bit text glyphs, a debugger HUD, damage as console.log lines.

### Controls

Keyboard: move WASD or arrows, actions J and K, shift Q (`<<`) and E (`>>`), Breakpoint Space, Watch R (hold), pause Esc or P. Gamepad: left stick or D-pad, X and A, LB and RB, RT for Breakpoint, Y for Watch, Start to pause. Title and pause screens: P practice, B debug build (an assist), F fullscreen, M mute (on pause). Full tables: README on GitHub (https://github.com/basteez/the-debugger-0x3-wild-shift#controls).

Tip: click once to enable audio if you play on a pad.

### Run length and platform

Plays in the browser, no install. A full run takes 10 to 15 minutes. Keyboard or gamepad.

### AI use

The code and the documentation of this game were written with an AI coding assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), under the direction of Tiziano Basile, who set the design, made the calls and reviewed the result. No generative image, music or voice model made any asset: the glyph font is bitmap data in an in-repo script (`game/tools/gen_glyph_font.py`), the sound effects and the placeholder music loops are synthesised by in-repo scripts (`game/tools/gen_sfx.py`, `game/tools/generate_placeholder_music.py`), and the rubber duck's voice is made by mumble-speech, Tiziano Basile's own library (live, or as pre-rendered clips made with it). The final music tracks are being made by Tiziano Basile in BeepBox.
